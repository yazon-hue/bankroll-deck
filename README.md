# Bankroll — pitch deck

Pitch deck for **Bankroll ($BANKROLL)**: a memecoin prop firm on Solana. Traders pay a small fee and pass a two-phase demo challenge (+25% then +50% targets, 5% daily and 20% total loss limits, 40% best-day rule). Funded accounts stay on demo with capped payouts at an 80% split; after five payouts, traders are reviewed for a real-money Live Desk account. A public USDC payout vault, backed by token stakers, pays every funded trader. "Bankroll" is a working name.

A single-file web deck. Navigate with arrow keys, scroll, swipe, or the on-screen arrows. Add `#14` to the URL to open a specific slide (slide 14 is the revenue scenarios). On a phone it reads best in landscape.

## Deploy to GitHub Pages

1. In the repo, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**, then choose `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
3. The deck will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` within a minute or two.

## Notes

- Revenue figures are illustrative scenarios, not forecasts. The 30% payout-and-cost figure is an assumption based on industry pass rates.
